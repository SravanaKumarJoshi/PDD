# Medical Claims Checklist

To comply with ethical standards and regulatory frameworks (e.g., FDA/MDR), the project strictly enforces boundaries on what can and cannot be claimed regarding the Polysaccharide ML Pipeline and Android Application.

## What We CAN Claim (Current Stage)
We currently possess evidence for **Technical/Engineering Validation** only.
- ✅ **Data Provenance:** "Trained entirely on expertly curated, real-world data from recognized chemical ontologies (PubChem, ChEMBL)."
- ✅ **Engineering Safety Gating:** "The system utilizes automated confidence thresholding and data-leakage suppression."
- ✅ **Taxonomic Accuracy (In-silico):** "Demonstrated 98.4% accuracy on held-out retrospective data splits from chemical databases."
- ✅ **Decision Support Capability:** "Functions as an educational decision-support tool backed by a peer-reviewed knowledge base."

## What We CANNOT Claim (Strictly Forbidden)
We do not possess the clinical evidence to legally or ethically make the following claims.
- ❌ **"Clinical-grade ready" or "Approved for clinical use"**
- ❌ **"Diagnostic"** (e.g., "Diagnoses carbohydrate deficiencies," "Detects pathogenic polysaccharides in patients")
- ❌ **"Safe for doctors" or "Safe for patient care"**
- ❌ **"Treatment recommendations"** (e.g., "Recommends Heparin dosing," "Suggests Alginate for this wound")
- ❌ **"Clinically proven" or "Validated in hospitals"**

## Evidence Required to Upgrade Claims
To move beyond "Technical Validation" and claim "Clinical Efficacy" or "Diagnostic Readiness," the following milestones must be achieved:
1. **Prospective Clinical Study:** The model must be evaluated in a real-world clinical or laboratory environment on fresh, sequential samples, demonstrating non-inferiority to existing standard-of-care analysis.
2. **True External Validation:** Testing on datasets entirely independent of the training data origin (e.g., data from a different hospital network or a different continent), proving generalization without geographic or institutional bias.
3. **Formal Bias and Fairness Audit:** Statistical proof that the model performs equitably across different molecular preparations, extraction methods, and edge cases.
4. **Regulatory Clearance:** Formal submission and clearance (e.g., 510(k) or De Novo pathway) acknowledging the intended use statement and clinical evidence by a body such as the FDA.
