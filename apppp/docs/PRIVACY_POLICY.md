# Privacy Policy

**BioPolymer AI Screening Platform**

*Last updated: March 2026*

## Overview

BioPolymer AI Screening Platform ("the App") is a research decision-support tool that helps researchers and engineers identify suitable natural polysaccharides for biomedical packaging applications. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information (Optional)
- **Email address** — if you choose to create an account for cloud sync
- **Authentication tokens** — managed by Firebase Authentication

### Usage Data (Opt-In Only)
- App feature usage patterns (anonymous)
- Crash reports (anonymous)
- This data is only collected if you explicitly enable analytics in Settings

### Research Data
- **Requirements inputs** — the material property requirements you enter
- **Screening results** — AI-generated recommendations
- These are stored locally on your device in encrypted storage
- If you enable cloud sync, this data is also stored on our servers

## How We Use Information

- To provide material screening and recommendation services
- To sync your projects across devices (if opted in)
- To improve the app through anonymous usage analytics (if opted in)

## Data Storage & Security

- Local data is stored in an encrypted SQLite database on your device
- Server data is stored in PostgreSQL with encrypted connections (TLS)
- Authentication is handled by Firebase/Supabase with industry-standard security
- We do not store passwords directly

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Export** all your data via Settings → Export My Data
- **Delete** all local data via Settings → Delete All Local Data
- **Opt out** of analytics at any time via Settings → Privacy
- **Delete your account** by contacting us at [contact email]

## Data Sharing

- We do **not** sell your data to third parties
- We do **not** share your research data with anyone
- Anonymous, aggregated usage statistics may be used for product improvement

## Important Disclaimer

This app is a **research decision-support tool only**. It does not constitute:
- Medical advice
- Regulatory guidance  
- Clinical recommendations

All material recommendations should be independently verified with primary literature and regulatory requirements.

## Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the app and on our website.
